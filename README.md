# Project Name: Bazar Dor

## Description

### Bazar-Dor — Real-Time Market Price Tracker
Bazar-Dor is a web application designed to help users track market prices of everyday products in different local markets. It provides price comparisons across markets and displays the minimum, maximum, and average prices of products, helping users understand price variations and make informed purchasing decisions.

The platform also helps users identify which markets offer lower prices and monitor whether product prices are increasing or decreasing. By making market price information easier to access, Bazar-Dor aims to help consumers make smarter shopping decisions and manage their daily expenses more effectively.

## Technologies
- TypeScript
- Next.js
- Tailwind Css
- Better-Auth

## Features

- **Real-Time Product Prices:** View the latest available prices of everyday products in different local markets.
- **Market Demand Insights:** Explore products that are in high demand in the market, based on available demand data.
- **Category-Based Product Browsing:** Browse products across different categories and compare their prices in various local markets.
- **Minimum, Maximum & Average Prices:** View price summaries to understand price variations across markets.
- **Price Trend Tracking:** Identify whether product prices are increasing or decreasing over time.
- **Price-Based Sorting:** Sort products by price from lowest to highest or highest to lowest.
- **Modern & Responsive UI:** Enjoy a clean, user-friendly interface optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
